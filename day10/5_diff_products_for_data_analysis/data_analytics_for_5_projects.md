# Data Analysis for 5 Different Projects

## Objective

Select 5 unique real-world problems from different domains and perform data analysis on each of them using the following 4 types of analytics:

1. **Descriptive Analysis** – What happened?
2. **Diagnostic Analysis** – Why did it happen?
3. **Predictive Analysis** – What might happen next?
4. **Prescriptive Analysis** – What should we do?

The main aim is to study historical data, identify patterns, understand the reasons behind changes, predict future outcomes, and suggest possible solutions based on the analysis.

---

# 1. Reservoir Water Inflow and Storage Analysis

**Domain:** Water Resource Management

### Problem Statement
Analyze reservoir inflow, outflow, rainfall, and water storage levels to understand water availability, predict future inflow, and improve water management decisions.

### Data Required
- Historical reservoir inflow and outflow
- Daily or monthly rainfall data
- Reservoir storage capacity and water levels
- Temperature and seasonal information
- Historical drought and flood records

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- How much water entered the reservoir over the past few years?
- During which months was the inflow highest and lowest?
- How did reservoir storage levels change across different seasons?
- What were the trends in rainfall and water usage?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why did the reservoir inflow suddenly increase or decrease?
- How did changes in rainfall affect water availability?
- Did higher temperatures and evaporation contribute to lower storage levels?
- What caused unexpected water shortages or overflow situations?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict reservoir inflow for the upcoming days or months.
- Estimate future reservoir storage levels based on rainfall forecasts.
- Identify the possibility of water shortages or reservoir overflow.
- Forecast water availability during upcoming summer and monsoon seasons.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest suitable water release schedules based on predicted inflow.
- Recommend water conservation measures during low-storage periods.
- Identify when early flood warnings may be required.
- Plan water allocation for agriculture, industries, and households.

# 2. Human Health and Lifestyle Risk Analysis

**Domain:** Healthcare and Preventive Health

### Problem Statement
Analyze health indicators and lifestyle habits to understand how daily activities affect overall health and predict the possibility of developing lifestyle-related diseases.

### Data Required
- Age, BMI, and blood pressure
- Blood glucose and cholesterol levels
- Sleep duration and sleep quality
- Daily physical activity and exercise
- Food habits and lifestyle information
- Medical history and existing health conditions

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- What percentage of individuals have high blood pressure or abnormal glucose levels?
- How do sleep duration and physical activity vary across age groups?
- What are the common health issues observed in the dataset?
- How have health indicators changed over time?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why do certain age groups show higher health risks?
- Is lack of physical activity associated with increased BMI?
- How are poor sleep patterns related to health indicators?
- Which lifestyle factors are associated with unhealthy glucose or cholesterol levels?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict the risk of developing diabetes or hypertension.
- Estimate future BMI or blood pressure trends.
- Identify individuals who may require preventive health monitoring.
- Predict potential health risks based on lifestyle patterns.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest improvements in physical activity, sleep, and dietary habits.
- Recommend preventive screening based on identified risk factors.
- Identify lifestyle changes that may help reduce future health risks.
- Suggest when individuals should consult healthcare professionals.

# 3. Urban Air Quality and Pollution Analysis

**Domain:** Environmental Monitoring

### Problem Statement
Analyze air pollution levels in urban areas to identify pollution patterns, study the factors contributing to poor air quality, and predict future air quality conditions.

### Data Required
- Air Quality Index (AQI)
- PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations
- Temperature, humidity, and wind speed
- Traffic density and vehicle movement
- Industrial emission data
- Historical air pollution records

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- How did air quality change over the past few years?
- Which months recorded the highest pollution levels?
- Which areas experienced poor air quality most frequently?
- What were the daily and seasonal trends in PM2.5 and PM10 concentrations?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why did pollution levels increase during certain periods?
- How did traffic density affect air quality?
- What role did weather conditions play in pollution accumulation?
- Were pollution spikes associated with industrial activity or seasonal events?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict AQI levels for the next 24 hours or 7 days.
- Forecast periods with a higher possibility of severe pollution.
- Identify locations likely to experience worsening air quality.
- Estimate future pollution levels using weather and historical data.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest traffic management measures during high-pollution periods.
- Recommend pollution alerts for sensitive groups.
- Identify areas where emission-control measures are required.
- Suggest suitable timings for outdoor activities based on predicted air quality.

# 4. Railway Delay and Network Congestion Analysis

**Domain:** Transportation and Public Infrastructure

### Problem Statement
Analyze railway arrival times, departure times, delays, and network conditions to understand the causes of train delays and predict future disruptions.

### Data Required
- Scheduled and actual arrival/departure times
- Train routes and station details
- Historical train delay records
- Weather conditions
- Passenger or train traffic density
- Railway maintenance and disruption records

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- How many trains arrived or departed late?
- Which routes experienced the most frequent delays?
- What was the average delay duration?
- During which hours or seasons were delays more common?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why were certain routes delayed more frequently?
- Did weather conditions contribute to train delays?
- How did congestion at major railway stations affect train movement?
- Were delays associated with track maintenance or operational disruptions?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict the expected delay duration of a train.
- Identify routes with a higher probability of future delays.
- Forecast congestion during peak travel periods.
- Predict possible disruptions based on weather and traffic patterns.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest improvements in train scheduling.
- Recommend alternative platform or route allocation when feasible.
- Identify suitable time periods for railway maintenance.
- Suggest early passenger notifications for predicted delays.

# 5. Agricultural Crop Yield and Soil Health Analysis

**Domain:** Agriculture and Food Security

### Problem Statement
Analyze crop production, soil conditions, rainfall, and weather patterns to understand factors affecting agricultural yield and predict future crop productivity.

### Data Required
- Historical crop yield and production data
- Rainfall and temperature records
- Soil pH and nutrient levels
- Soil moisture measurements
- Irrigation details
- Fertilizer usage and crop types

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- How did crop yield change over the past few seasons?
- Which crops produced the highest and lowest yields?
- How did soil moisture and nutrient levels vary?
- What was the relationship between rainfall patterns and crop productivity?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why did crop yield decrease during certain seasons?
- Did insufficient rainfall affect crop production?
- How were soil nutrient deficiencies associated with lower yield?
- Did extreme weather conditions contribute to crop damage?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict crop yield for the upcoming growing season.
- Estimate soil moisture levels based on future weather conditions.
- Identify crops at greater risk of low productivity.
- Forecast potential water requirements for irrigation.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest suitable crops based on soil conditions and climate.
- Recommend irrigation schedules based on soil moisture predictions.
- Identify areas requiring soil nutrient improvement.
- Suggest preventive measures against predicted crop stress.

## Common Workflow for All 5 Projects

1. **Data Collection:** Collect datasets from reliable sources related to the selected domain.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Handle missing values, duplicate records, incorrect values, and inconsistent formats.
3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Study distributions, trends, correlations, and unusual patterns through visualizations.
4. **Descriptive Analysis:** Summarize historical events and identify what happened.
5. **Diagnostic Analysis:** Investigate possible reasons behind observed patterns using relevant data.
6. **Predictive Analysis:** Apply suitable statistical or machine learning models to estimate future outcomes.
7. **Prescriptive Analysis:** Recommend actions based on the findings, predictions, and practical constraints.
8. **Results and Visualization:** Present the final observations using graphs, dashboards, and a short analysis report.

## Expected Outcome

By completing these 5 projects, we can understand how the four types of data analytics are applied to different real-world problems. Each project will cover historical analysis, identification of contributing factors, prediction of possible future conditions, and recommendations to support better decisions.
