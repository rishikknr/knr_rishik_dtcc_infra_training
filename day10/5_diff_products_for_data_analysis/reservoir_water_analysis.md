
# 1. Reservoir Water Inflow and Storage Analysis

**Domain:** Water Resource Management

### Problem Statement
Analyze reservoir inflow, outflow, rainfall, and water storage levels to understand water availability, predict future inflow, and improve water management decisions.

### Data Required
- Historical reservoir inflow and outflow
- Daily or monthly rainfall data
- Reservoir storage capacity and water levels
- Temperature and seasonal information
- Historical drought and flood records

### 1. Descriptive Analysis – What happened?
- How much water entered the reservoir over the past few years?
- During which months was the inflow highest and lowest?
- How did reservoir storage levels change across different seasons?
- What were the trends in rainfall and water usage?

### 2. Diagnostic Analysis – Why did it happen?
- Why did the reservoir inflow suddenly increase or decrease?
- How did changes in rainfall affect water availability?
- Did higher temperatures and evaporation contribute to lower storage levels?
- What caused unexpected water shortages or overflow situations?

### 3. Predictive Analysis – What might happen next?
- Predict reservoir inflow for the upcoming days or months.
- Estimate future reservoir storage levels based on rainfall forecasts.
- Identify the possibility of water shortages or reservoir overflow.
- Forecast water availability during upcoming summer and monsoon seasons.

### 4. Prescriptive Analysis – What should we do?
- Suggest suitable water release schedules based on predicted inflow.
- Recommend water conservation measures during low-storage periods.
- Identify when early flood warnings may be required.
- Plan water allocation for agriculture, industries, and households.
