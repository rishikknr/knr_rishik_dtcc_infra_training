# Five Different Visuals in Power BI

## Objective
To represent the e-commerce sales dataset using five different types of Power BI visuals and compare the information shown by each chart.

## Visuals Created

### 1. Clustered Column Chart — Total Profit by Category
- **Axis:** Category
- **Values:** Total Profit
- Used vertical columns to compare profit across product categories.

### 2. Donut Chart — Quantity by Category
- **Legend:** Category
- **Values:** Sum of Quantity
- Used a donut chart to show each category's share of the total quantity sold.

### 3. Scatter Chart — Quantity and Sales by Region and Brand
- **X-axis:** Sum of Quantity
- **Y-axis:** Total Sales
- **Details/Legend:** Region and Brand
- Used a scatter chart to compare quantity sold with sales and identify differences across groups.

### 4. Clustered Bar Chart — Gross Sales by Customer Segment
- **Axis:** Customer Segment
- **Values:** Gross Sales
- Used horizontal bars to compare gross sales from Consumer, Small Business and Corporate customers.

### 5. Line Chart — Quantity by Brand and Category
- **X-axis:** Brand
- **Y-axis:** Sum of Quantity
- **Legend:** Category
- Used multiple lines to compare product quantities across brands and categories.

## Result
Five different chart types were created from the same e-commerce dataset. Each visual presents a different comparison: category profit, quantity distribution, sales versus quantity, customer-segment sales, and brand-wise quantities.
